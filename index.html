Acupuncture Practice Manager Full-stack clinic management system

Feb – Oct 2026

  • Built a practice management system for an acupuncture clinic, with an appointment calendar, client records, treatment notes, and PDF invoices that meet Australian private health fund, TAC and WorkSafe claim requirements.
  • Protected health and banking data with AES-256-GCM encryption of treatment notes and bank details, locked note revisions, revocable sessions, two-step login with TOTP (implemented from RFC 6238), trusted devices, and admin, practitioner and reception roles with an audit log.

Aji Web KataGo running entirely in the browser

Nov 2026

  • Ported Aji to a static website that runs KataGo's neural network on the player's own device through ONNX Runtime Web, on the GPU (WebGPU) or the CPU (WebAssembly), with no server.
  • Reimplemented KataGo's board rules, ladder reading, network inputs and tree search in TypeScript, and wrote a converter from KataGo network files to ONNX, including transformer-attention models.
  • Checked it against desktop KataGo: the small network matches to 4 decimal places on 15 test positions, and search win rates agree within about 1%.
  • Includes 900 tsumego problems, SGF open and save, and a phone layout that can be installed to the home screen. Deployed on Cloudflare Pages.

Live Translate Real-time local speech translation

Sep 2026

  • Built a captioning app that captures audio from a browser tab, transcribes it with local speech-recognition models and translates it into English with a local Gemma 4 LLM. Audio never leaves the computer.
  • Streamed 16 kHz audio from an AudioWorklet over a WebSocket gateway to six swappable speech-recognition pipelines (SenseVoice, Whisper Large v3, Qwen3-ASR, ReazonSpeech, Kushinada) with voice-activity detection.
  • Shows partial captions that update while someone is speaking, then a final translation after each pause. Translation is served by llama.cpp, and every model service listens only on localhost.
